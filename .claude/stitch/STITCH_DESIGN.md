## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authentic, warm, and clinical perspective for veterinary medicine in Çanakkale. It moves away from childlike pet-clinic stereotypes—such as cartoon paw vectors, generic clip-art, or sterile laboratory blues—and instead embraces an editorial, grounded, and agrarian-informed aesthetic. The visual tone bridges three core disciplines: companion pet clinical medicine, extensive field and livestock care, and apicultural and colony health consulting.

The design movement combines **warm editorial minimalism** with **grounded agrarian elegance**. The atmosphere evokes rural dignity, disciplined medical confidence, and deep empathy through generous whitespace, tactile cream backdrops, dark pine forest surfaces, and sun-warmed terracotta call-to-actions. The physical presence of authentic photography—field injections, newborn calves in straw, and apiary inspections—anchors the interface in genuine craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in earth, pine canopy, sun-baked clay, and unbleached organic paper:

- **Primary (`#18352f`)**: A deep, restorative forest green serving as the cornerstone of medical authority, anchor panels, metric bands, and high-impact containers.
- **Secondary (`#d97b3f`)**: Terracotta orange, used strictly for primary actions, emergency markers, and active interactive highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#24453d`)**: A softened evergreen, supporting visual transitions, dark-mode gradient stops, and hovered interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#f6f1e8`)**: Organic warm linen cream providing the master canvas background, eliminating screen glare and cold clinical sensations.

### Surface Hierarchy & Named Tokens
- **`surface` (`#fffdf9`)**: Premium milk-white container fill for clinical service cards, dialogs, and primary forms.
- **`surface-strong` (`#f1e6d5`)**: Tinted oat-beige background for secondary info modules, badge pills, and interactive callouts.
- **`text-main` (`#16302a`)**: Deep pine-black for uncompromising typographic contrast without harsh absolute-black rendering.
- **`text-muted` (`#5f6f68`)**: Desaturated sage grey-green for descriptions, captions, and supportive metadata.
- **`accent-soft` (`#f3d2bc`)**: Peach-terracotta wash for badge backgrounds and active input ring highlights.
- **`logo-red` (`#cc1418`)**: Reserved exclusively for the registered brand logomark. It is never applied to UI components, buttons, or typography.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances the gravitas of a traditional academic journal with the high legibility demanded by modern healthcare applications:

- **Fraunces** handles all display headings, quote highlights, and primary section anchors. With soft optical curves and balanced serifs, it instills warmth, literary trust, and clinical heritage.
- **Manrope** powers body copy, interface badges, metric tickers, and button labels. It offers geometric clarity, uniform kerning, and distinct figure rendering for technical field data and scheduling.
- **Eyebrows & Section Markers**: Rendered in `label-sm`, all caps, using Manrope 700 with generous tracking (`0.08em`), paired with a leading dot or terracotta slash for editorial punctuation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content container width of 1240px. The visual rhythm emphasizes generous vertical whitespace, allowing clinical text and high-resolution documentary photography to coexist without visual clutter.

- **Desktop (≥1024px)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters (`1.5rem`), outer margins of 48px (`3rem`). Section blocks use `space-3xl` (96px) vertical separation.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, outer margins of 32px (`2rem`).
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters (`1rem`), 20px margins (`1.25rem`). Three-pillar services reflow from horizontal trio cards into single-column vertical cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved primarily through **tonal surface stacking** and **tactile borders**, avoiding synthetic dropshadows or heavy blur layers.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas)**: Baseline page background in neutral cream (`#f6f1e8`).
- **Layer 1 (Card Surface)**: Lifted using `#fffdf9` bordered with a 1px low-contrast line (`rgba(22, 48, 42, 0.08)`).
- **Ambient Floor Elevation**: When physical lifting is required (such as sticky booking bars or interactive service cards on hover), use a warm-tinted shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(24, 53, 47, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(24, 53, 47, 0.04)`.
- **Inverted Dark Anchor Modules**: Full-width bands utilizing `#18352f` with inner border hairline highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`) to anchor location stats and field commitments.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, balanced geometry (Level 2: Rounded). This avoids both sharp utilitarian edges and overly playful pill/bubble forms:

- **Cards & Visual Containers**: `16px` border-radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Input Fields & Action Buttons**: `10px` to `12px` border-radius (`rounded-md`).
- **Category Tags & Operational Status Chips**: Full pill `9999px` to emphasize discrete touch targets.
- **Photography Aspect Ratios**: Editorial `4:5` vertical framing for livestock and field work; cinematic `16:9` for clinic interiors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Randevu / CTA)**: Solid terracotta background (`#d97b3f`), white typography (`#fffdf9`), font-weight 700. On hover, shifts to darker terracotta (`#c26830`) with a smooth 150ms cubic-bezier transition.
- **Secondary Action (Hemen Ara / Telefon)**: Transparent surface framed by 1.5px solid forest green (`#18352f`) with `#18352f` text. On dark bands, this dynamically swaps to a warm-white outline (`#fffdf9`) with white text.
- **Text / Inline Action**: `#18352f` text with a 1px underline positioned with `text-underline-offset: 4px` accompanied by an inline right-arrow vector.

### Cards & Service Pillars
- Built on `surface` (`#fffdf9`) with a 1px border (`rgba(22, 48, 42, 0.08)`). Padding is fixed at `space-xl` (40px desktop, 24px mobile).
- Cards featuring photography feature full-bleed top imagery with rounded corners matching the container, separated by a hairline divider.
- Service pillar badges use `accent-soft` (`#f3d2bc`) with `#16302a` text.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill-shaped elements for metadata (e.g., "7/24 Acil Saha Desteği", "Arıcılık Danışmanlığı", "Çanakkale Merkez").
- Background: `#f1e6d5` (`surface-strong`), text color: `#16302a`, 12px font size, 600 weight, 6px vertical by 14px horizontal padding.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Background: `#fffdf9`. Border: 1.5px solid `#dcd4c7`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#18352f` with a 3px outer glow in `#f3d2bc` (`accent-soft`).
- Placeholder text in `#5f6f68` (`text-muted`).

### Emergency / Quick Contact Sticky Bar
- A fixed bottom bar for mobile viewports: background in `#18352f`, dividing call and route actions evenly with hairline borders, maintaining touch heights of minimum 48px.
